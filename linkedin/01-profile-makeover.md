## 2. Headline (220 char max)
This is the single most important line. It shows in search, comments, and DMs.
Don't just write your job title. Write **who you help + how**.

**Pick one (or blend):**

**A) Outcome-led**
> Senior Development Manager @ A2 Automation | Helping developers, consultants & data centres deliver reliable ICT, ELV, BMS, UPS (Vertiv/Liebert) & Axis CCTV — on spec, on time.

**B) Authority-led**
> ICT & ELV Systems Integration | Structured Cabling · Building Management · Vertiv/Liebert Critical Power · Axis IP Surveillance | Senior Development Manager, A2 Automation Sdn Bhd

**C) Problem-led**
> I help building & data-centre owners avoid downtime and rework — integrated ICT, ELV, BMS, critical power & CCTV | Senior Development Manager, A2 Automation

*(Recommended: A — it names the buyer, the solutions, and the promise.)*
