# 4-Week LinkedIn Content Calendar — A2 Automation

> Cadence: **3 posts/week** (Mon / Wed / Fri, ~9am MYT). Consistency beats volume.
> Rotate 5 content types so you're never "just selling":
>
> 1. **Educational** — teach buyers something (builds authority)
> 2. **Proof** — case study / project / result (builds trust)
> 3. **Insight/Opinion** — your take on an industry topic (builds personality)
> 4. **Behind-the-scenes** — team, install, commissioning (builds relatability)
> 5. **Soft CTA** — invite conversations (generates leads)
>
> Rule of thumb: for every 1 selling post, publish 4 that give value.
> Always end with a question — comments = reach.

## Posting tips that actually move the needle
- **First 2 lines = everything.** They decide who clicks "see more."
- **No outbound links in the post body** (LinkedIn suppresses them). Put links in the first comment.
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour** — it multiplies reach.
- **Tag relevant people/companies sparingly** (only if genuinely relevant).
- **Reuse:** every case study can become an educational post, a proof post, and a BTS post.
- **Track** which hooks get engagement and make more of those (see the lead tracker).
