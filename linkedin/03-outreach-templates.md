# Outreach Templates — A2 Automation

> ⚠️ Send these **manually**, personalised each time. Do NOT use automation tools —
> LinkedIn bans accounts for it, and generic blasts kill your reputation.
> Target ~10–20 thoughtful connections/day, max.
>
> The sequence that works: **Connect (no pitch) → Add value → Soft ask → Follow up.**
> Never pitch in the connection request. You're earning the right to a conversation.

---

## STEP 1 — Connection Requests (max 200–300 chars, NO pitch)

Personalise the first line every time. Reference something real (their post, company,
project, mutual connection).

**To an M&E Consultant**
```
Hi [Name], I work on the ICT/ELV & critical-power side of building projects at A2 Automation.
Always keen to connect with consultants shaping how buildings get specified. Would be good
to be connected.
```

**To a Property Developer / Project Director**
```
Hi [Name], I lead ICT/ELV & building-systems delivery at A2 Automation here in Malaysia.
I follow developers doing interesting work — [their project] caught my eye. Happy to connect.
```

**To a Main Contractor / QS**
```
Hi [Name], we handle the ICT/ELV/power packages on building projects at A2 Automation.
Always value connecting with contractors who care about the trades that don't show up until
commissioning. Let's connect.
```

**To a Data-Centre / Facilities Manager**
```
Hi [Name], I work with data-centre and facilities teams on critical power/cooling (Vertiv/
Liebert) and surveillance (Axis) at A2 Automation. Would be glad to connect with someone in
your field.
```

**Warm (mutual connection / same event)**
```
Hi [Name], we're both connected to [mutual] / both at [event]. I lead ICT/ELV delivery at
A2 Automation — would be good to connect.
```

---

## STEP 2 — After they accept (value first, NO pitch yet)

Send within 24h of acceptance. Thank + give something, don't sell.

**General value message**
```
Thanks for connecting, [Name]! No pitch from me — I mostly share practical stuff on ICT/ELV,
building systems and critical power on here.

Out of curiosity, what kind of projects is [company] focused on this year? Always interested
in what people in [their field] are working on.
```

**With a useful resource**
```
Appreciate the connect, [Name]. Since you're in [field], you might find this useful — we put
together a short checklist on specifying ELV/CCTV so it doesn't get value-engineered into
problems later. Happy to send it over if it's relevant. Either way, good to be connected!
```

---

## STEP 3 — Soft ask (only after 1–2 value touches, or if they engage)

**When there's a real trigger (new project, they liked your post, etc.)**
```
Hi [Name], saw [trigger — new development / your post on X]. On projects like that, the
ICT/ELV + power side is usually where timelines and budgets get tested.

We do exactly that at A2 Automation — integrated cabling, CCTV (Axis), BMS and critical power
(Vertiv/Liebert), one accountable partner instead of five vendors.

Worth a quick 15-min call to see if we'd be useful on anything you've got coming up?
```

**Low-pressure version**
```
Hi [Name], not sure of your timing, but if any upcoming project needs ICT/ELV, surveillance,
BMS or UPS/cooling done properly, I'd love to be on your radar. Even a quick chat so you know
what we do — no obligation. Would that be useful?
```

---

## STEP 4 — Follow-ups (if no reply — space them out, then stop)

**Follow-up 1 (after ~5–7 days)**
```
Hi [Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried. Even if there's nothing now,
happy to share a capability one-pager so we're a known option when a project comes up. Want
me to send it?
```

**Follow-up 2 (after ~2 weeks — final, gracious)**
```
Hi [Name], I'll leave it here so I'm not a nuisance 🙂. If ICT/ELV, CCTV, BMS or critical
power ever lands on your desk, I'm a message away. Wishing you a strong [year/quarter]!
```

*(No reply after two follow-ups = stop. Keep engaging with their posts instead — stay visible, not pushy.)*

## Responding to inbound interest (someone comments "info"/"2026")
```
Thanks [Name]! Quick question so I point you right — is this for a new build, an upgrade,
or a specific system (CCTV / BMS / power)? I'll send something relevant and we can set up a
short call if it's useful.
```

---

## The golden rules
1. **Personalise line 1** — no exceptions. Generic = ignored or reported.
2. **Give before you ask** — 2 value touches minimum before a pitch.
3. **Every message ends with a question** — make replying easy.
4. **Cap volume** — 10–20 connects/day, quality over quantity, protect the account.
5. **Track everything** — log every prospect and touch in the lead tracker.
6. **When they say no or go quiet — be gracious.** Reputation compounds; desperation repels.
