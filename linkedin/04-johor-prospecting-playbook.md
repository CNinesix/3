## Part 1 — Why Johor, right now
Johor is one of the strongest infrastructure markets in the region — which means real
demand for exactly what A2 sells:

- **Data-centre boom** — Sedenak Tech Park / Kulai / Sedenak Tech Valley are drawing
  hyperscale and colocation builds. Data centres = critical power (UPS), precision
  cooling (Vertiv/Liebert), structured cabling, and surveillance. This is your #1 target.
- **Iskandar Malaysia / JB developments** — commercial towers, mixed-use, hospitals,
  campuses = ELV, BMS, CCTV, access control.
- **Manufacturing corridors** — Pasir Gudang, Senai, Tebrau, Kulai, Tanjung Langsat =
  factories needing surveillance, access control, network infrastructure, UPS.
- **JS-SEZ (Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone)** — cross-border investment pulling
  in new facilities and upgrades.

**Takeaway:** lead with **data centres** and **new commercial/industrial builds**.

## Part 4 — Company targeting (find the company, then the people)
Instead of only searching people, search **companies** in Johor, then look at *their*
employees. Company types to search:

- Data-centre operators & colocation providers (Sedenak / Kulai)
- Property developers active in Iskandar / JB
- Main contractors & M&E contractors
- Manufacturing plants (electronics, semiconductor, F&B, logistics) in Pasir Gudang /
  Senai / Tebrau
- Hospitals, universities, shopping malls, hotels (facilities-heavy buildings)
- M&E and building-services consulting firms

**How:** LinkedIn search → **Companies** → filter Location = Johor → open a company →
**People** tab → filter by the job titles in Part 2.

## Part 6 — Qualifying questions (once in conversation)
Don't pitch products — diagnose needs. Ask:
1. "Any new builds, fit-outs, or upgrades planned in the next 6–12 months?"
2. "Who currently handles your ELV / surveillance / building systems?"
3. "Any pain points with your current setup — downtime, blind spots, systems that don't talk?"
4. "Are you specifying for a specific project, or building a preferred-vendor list?"

Their answers tell you whether to talk CCTV, BMS, cabling, or critical power.
