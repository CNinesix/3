## Your first week (30 min/day)
- **Day 1:** Update headline + About (from file 01). This alone lifts every other effort.
- **Day 2:** Add banner, Featured items, turn on services.
- **Day 3:** Post Week-1 Monday content. Reply to all comments within the hour.
- **Day 4:** Send 10 connection requests (file 03, Step 1). Personalise each.
- **Day 5:** Post Week-1 Wednesday content. Message anyone who accepted (Step 2).
- **Ongoing:** 3 posts/week + 10–15 personalised connects/day. Track results.

## The 90-day mindset
LinkedIn business is a compounding game, not a switch. Months 1–2 build credibility and
visibility; leads typically start landing in months 2–3 as your name keeps showing up in
front of the right people. Consistency is the whole strategy.
