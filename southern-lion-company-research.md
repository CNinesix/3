# Southern Lion Sdn Bhd — Company Deep Research

*Johor, Malaysia · Consumer goods / FMCG manufacturer*
*Report date: 2026-07-22*

---

## Executive Summary

Southern Lion Sdn Bhd is a leading Malaysian fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG)
company, established in **1987** as an **equal-sharing (50/50) joint venture**
between **Lion Corporation of Japan** and **Lam Soon (M) Bhd**, headquartered in
**Johor Bahru, Johor**. The company manufactures and markets fabric care, oral
care, beauty care, and home/living care products, drawing on the portfolio and
technology of its Japanese parent — a 1918-founded multinational maker of
detergents, soaps, oral-hygiene products, and OTC pharmaceuticals.

Southern Lion holds **ISO 9001**, **ISO 14001** (Environmental Management), and
**GMP** (toiletry goods) certifications, and runs long-standing oral-health CSR
initiatives — the **Lion Hygiene Program** (est. 1998) in schools and
collaborations with the Malaysian Ministry of Health and dental societies
promoting brands such as **Systema** and **KODOMO**. It sits within a broader
network of Lion Corporation Southeast Asian joint ventures spanning Thailand,
Indonesia (PT Lion Wings), and the Philippines (Peerless Lion), giving it a
regional strategic context alongside its domestic Malaysian market position.

> leadership, and detailed plant data could **not** be independently verified
> and are flagged accordingly. Primary sources (`lion.co.jp`,

## 2. Ownership & Joint-Venture Structure

Southern Lion is jointly and equally owned by:

- **Lion Corporation (Japan)** — the Japanese FMCG multinational, providing
  brands, formulations, and technology.
- **Lam Soon (M) Bhd** — the Malaysian partner, part of the Lam Soon Group,
  providing local market presence and distribution.

**High confidence.** Note a common imprecision: Wikipedia lists Southern Lion
under Lion Corporation's *"subsidiaries,"* but a 50/50 equal joint venture is
technically an **associate / JV**, not a controlled subsidiary of either parent.

- **Lion Corporation (Thailand)** — JV founded 1967, currently ~50% Lion Corp /

  Manufacturing Corporation.

## 8. Sustainability & CSR

**High confidence.** Southern Lion runs long-standing oral-health CSR programs:

- **Lion Hygiene Program** (est. 1998) — promotes oral-healthcare habits in
  nursery and elementary schools, targeting children aged 4–12, developed with
  the Lion Foundation for Dental Health.
- **Ministry of Health / dental-society collaboration** — promotes oral-care
  brands (Systema, KODOMO) at dental clinics, distributing product samples.
- The company's own site describes a related **Lion Dental Health Program**
  (est. 1998) with Government Dental Clinics and the Malaysian Dental Therapists
  Association, citing **57 collaborative programs in 2025**.

Environmental posture is anchored by its **ISO 14001** certification.

This report was produced by a fan-out research harness: the question was
decomposed into 6 search angles, run as parallel web searches, deduplicated to
19 fetched sources, from which 9 falsifiable claims were extracted. Each claim
underwent 3-vote adversarial verification (a claim needed 2 of 3 refutations to
be killed). **All 9 claims were confirmed 3-0; none were refuted.**

**Access caveat.** Both `lion.co.jp` and `southernlion.com.my` returned HTTP 403
to direct fetch, so their primary-source text was verified via search-engine
index snapshots. Text reproduced consistently, but page currency (especially the
undated certification statement) could not be confirmed live. Segment
